1. What is dgem? (Product & Architecture Summary)

System-1 Decision Engine + Policy-as-Code

① O(1) Parallel Canvas Readout

Instead of generating tokens left-to-right ($O(N)$), dgem places multiple [MASK] slots on DiffusionGemma's bidirectional canvas and resolves up to 8–12 orthogonal decision fields in a single ~150–400ms forward pass.

② Exact Shannon Entropy Gate ($H$)

Every decision slot emits an exact restricted-softmax probability distribution and Shannon entropy ($H = -\sum p_k \ln p_k$). Low-entropy cases exit immediately; high-entropy edge cases escalate to Gemini (the "Triage Nurse vs. Specialist" pattern).

③ Wide-Canvas & Multi-Slot Adapter

Standard diffusion servers fail on $K > 26$ options ([A-Z] token limit) or $M > 10$ slots. dgem adds 2-Stage Bracket Tournament Routing ($K \le 255$) + Multi-Slot Batching + Slot Temperature Scaling ($T^*$).
