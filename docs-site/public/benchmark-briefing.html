What is dgem? Architecture & Mathematical Formulation

Unlike autoregressive models that generate tokens sequentially (O(N)), dgem places multiple [MASK] decision slots on DiffusionGemma's bidirectional canvas and extracts exact restricted-softmax distributions in O(1) forward passes (~150–400 ms on Cloud Run GPU).

1. Parallel Canvas Readout

Evaluates up to 8–12 orthogonal rubric slots simultaneously in a single forward pass. Each slot m emits calibrated probabilities pm,k over its allowed option set Vm.

2. Normalized Entropy Gate

Computes cardinality-normalized Shannon entropy H̃m = Hm / ln|Vm| ∈ [0, 1]. Prompts with H̃m < 0.16 exit immediately at Stage 1; ambiguous cases escalate to Gemini.

3. Wide-Canvas & Batch Adapter

Eliminates HTTP 422 capacity rejections on K > 26 options (2-Stage Bracket Tournament Routing up to K = 255) and M > 10 slots (Multi-Slot Batching) with T* scaling.

EXP-13: Permutation Sensitivity, Null-Prior De-Biasing & O(1) Dual-Mirror Canvas

Why isn't single-pass Shannon entropy (H̃) always sufficient on its own? Because a distribution over option letters measures token-slot confidence under a fixed option order π: z(ℓk | X, π) = s(oπ(k) | X) + bpos(k). When a task is genuinely ambiguous (s(o1) ≈ s(o2)), a strong positional prior bpos(0) toward Slot 'A' inflates whichever option sits in 'A', collapsing single-pass entropy and risking a False Early Exit.

EXP-13B: Content-Free Positional Null Priors (p0)

When dgemma evaluates a content-free state ([REDACTED / CONTENT-FREE CALIBRATION PROBE]), its probabilities are heavily skewed toward Slot 0 ('A'):

Cardinality (K) Slot 'A' Slot 'B' Slot 'C' Slot 'D' 'A' Bias
K = 2 (Binary) 88.3% 11.7% — — 1.77×
K = 3 (3-Way NLI) 78.3% 8.6% 13.1% — 2.35×
K = 4 (4-Way MCQ) 49.3% 4.8% 16.3% 29.5% 1.97×

EXP-13C: O(1) Single-Pass Dual-Mirror Canvas (reads=1)

Because DiffusionGemma denoises all canvas slots in one bidirectional GPU forward pass, dgem places both decision_fwd ([o1 … oK]) and decision_rev ([oK … o1]) on the same diffusion canvas:

  • Zero Latency Penalty (125 ms vs 129 ms 1-Slot): Both mirrored slots resolve simultaneously in reads=1.
  • Exact Reversal Invariance (0.0% Flip Rate): First-slot and last-slot positional biases cancel algebraically (bpos(k) + bpos(K-1-k)).
  • Catches False Early Exits via Mirror TVD: On ChaosNLI human-split cases (perm_06, perm_08) where single-pass H̃ = 0.007–0.031 is fooled by 'A'-bias, Mirror TVD spikes 66.8× (0.274 vs 0.004) and Cyclic JSD jumps 157.8×.

Chronological Execution Order: How dgem + DiffusionGemma Work Together in One Request (~125–300 ms)

For non-ML stakeholders, think of DiffusionGemma as a high-speed judge that reads an entire page of checkboxes at once, and dgem as the ballot designer, digital scale, and traffic controller wrapping that judge in five strict steps:

Step 1 · Pre-Pass (0.1 ms)
Ballot Design & Mirroring

dgem prints the ballot twice on the same canvas: once forward (A..D) and once reversed (D..A), or splits wide lists (>26) into brackets.

Step 2 · GPU Pass (~125 ms)
Diffusion Snapshot Read

DiffusionGemma evaluates all forward and mirrored slots simultaneously in one bidirectional GPU pass and emits raw letter scores.

Step 3 · Post-Pass 1 (0.1 ms)
Tare the Scale (Null-Prior)

dgem subtracts the model's built-in Box A habit (p0 = 49%–88% on blank input), cutting Brier error by up to 90.2%.

Step 4 · Post-Pass 2 (0.1 ms)
Mirror Merge & Temp Dial

dgem compares the forward & reversed ballots (Mirror TVD) and applies the Humility Dial (T* = 1.25–1.35) so 95% confidence = 95% accuracy.

Step 5 · Post-Pass 3 (Gate)
Fast-Exit or Escalate

If both ballots agree (H̃ < 0.16), dgem returns immediately (72% of traffic); if they clash, it escalates to Gemini (98.0% accuracy).

1. WHAT IS DGEM?
dgem is our zero-shot Decision Model serving, calibration, and policy-routing engine for Google DeepMind's DiffusionGemma (26B-A4B). Instead of generating conversational text token-by-token, dgem evaluates structured multi-slot rubrics (Policy-as-Template) in O(1) bidirectional forward passes (~125-400ms on Cloud Run GPU) and uses exact epistemic calibration to decide whether to return immediately or escalate ambiguous cases to Gemini.

2. BENCHMARK 1: JevBench v1.3.1 (EXP-11 — Adversarial Rule-Following & 4-Axis Calibration)

• Why Single-Pass Entropy Isn't Enough: Content-free null probes reveal a strong structural prior toward Slot 'A' (88.3% on K=2, 78.3% on K=3, 49.3% on K=4).
