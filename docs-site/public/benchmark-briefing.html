The current record for dgem, the Policy-as-Template decision engine on DiffusionGemma: JevBench v1.3.1 (231 items), the 50-item public calibration suite, the Decision Index panel, the confidence experiments EXP-14–17, the evaluation-judge study EXP-18, and the side-by-side validation of the v0.1.0 serving image. What changed since the 25 September version
The earlier briefing centred on the same-canvas Dual-Mirror and null-prior de-biasing. Same-session re-runs and follow-ups (EXP-14–17) showed null-prior helps one suite and hurts another, and the mirror's accuracy loss came from letter collisions; it adds little over hesitation. Hesitation gating plus a Gemini cascade is now the recommendation. Serving moved to Vertex G4 on a validated upstream-vLLM image (v0.1.0).

Confidence experiments (EXP-13 to EXP-17)

DiffusionGemma puts 88.3% / 78.3% / 49.3% of its probability on option A for blank 2 / 3 / 4-option questions. We tested request-time corrections (Invariant Decision Calibration). All JevBench runs below are one session each, 231 items.

ExperimentQuestionResultVerdict
EXP-14Do null-prior and the same-canvas mirror help, re-run in one session?Null-prior: 50-item Brier 0.147 (better), JevBench 0.293 (worse). Mirror with __mirror_rev: JevBench collapsed to 145; with __rev: 163–169.Suite-dependent
EXP-15Is the mirror damage letter collision?Lettered reversed slot 154; identical copy 184; digit-labelled reversed 182 (band 182–189). Same-letter items changed answer 67% of the time.Confirmed
EXP-16Do slot names matter?Single-slot ids: no. A second slot named __mirror_rev cost 19 items vs __rev.Use neutral ids
EXP-17Does a separate-pass mirror add signal?Partial Spearman 0.13 [0.03, 0.24], but two ordinary passes are similar; CV error detection +0.016 AUROC at most, 2× cost.Small effect
EXP-14Held-out temperature scalingJevBench ECE −24–33% (T* ≈ 1.5); no reliable gain on the 50-item suite.Optional
Recommendation
Gate on hesitation (normalized entropy, ~16%) and escalate hesitant answers to a person or Gemini. Keep null-prior and the mirror as research tools; if you use a mirror, use digit labels (--mirror-mode reversed-digits) and the __rev suffix.

05

Decision Index panel (EXP-12, 22 requests)

Bracket routing (> 26 options) and slot batching (> 10 questions) raised coverage from 16/22 to 22/22 and the panel index from 76.67 to 98.89. This is a coverage fix on a small panel; a full-suite run has not been made.

07

Limits

  • Small samples (50–231 items); cascade thresholds tuned on the evaluation items.
  • One forward pass can't reason step by step; the cascade covers that at the cost of escalation.
  • About 5% of per-item answers change across serving images; compare items within one image.
  • 26 options per slot; conditional questions add a pass; long multi-slot prompts are slower.
