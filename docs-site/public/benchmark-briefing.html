What is dgem? Architecture & Mathematical Formulation

Unlike autoregressive models that generate tokens sequentially (O(N)), dgem places multiple [MASK] decision slots on DiffusionGemma's bidirectional canvas and extracts exact restricted-softmax distributions in O(1) forward passes (~150–400 ms on Cloud Run GPU).

1. Parallel Canvas Readout

Evaluates up to 8–12 orthogonal rubric slots simultaneously in a single forward pass. Each slot m emits calibrated probabilities pm,k over its allowed option set Vm.

2. Normalized Entropy Gate

Computes cardinality-normalized Shannon entropy H̃m = Hm / ln|Vm| ∈ [0, 1]. Prompts with H̃m < 0.16 exit immediately at Stage 1; ambiguous cases escalate to Gemini.

3. Wide-Canvas & Batch Adapter

Eliminates HTTP 422 capacity rejections on K > 26 options (2-Stage Bracket Tournament Routing up to K = 255) and M > 10 slots (Multi-Slot Batching) with T* scaling.

Subject: dgem (DiffusionGemma 26B-A4B) — Unified Readout on JevBench v1.3.1 & jev-decision-index

1. WHAT IS DGEM?
dgem is our zero-shot Decision Model serving, calibration, and policy-routing engine for Google DeepMind's DiffusionGemma (26B-A4B). Instead of generating conversational text token-by-token, dgem evaluates structured multi-slot rubrics (Policy-as-Template) in O(1) bidirectional forward passes (~150-400ms on Cloud Run GPU) and uses exact Shannon entropy (H) to decide whether to return immediately or escalate ambiguous cases to Gemini.

2. BENCHMARK 1: JevBench v1.3.1 (EXP-11 — Adversarial Rule-Following & 4-Axis Calibration)
• Evaluates 231 tricky rule-following cases across 4 axes (Chance-Corrected Intelligence, 10-Bin ECE Calibration, Latency, Cost).
• Slot Temperature Scaling (T* = 1.35): Softens raw diffusion logit overconfidence without changing a single argmax answer, cutting 10-Bin ECE by 56.2% (0.0745 -> 0.0326) and lifting Calibration to 88.18 (76.79 4-Axis Composite, 97.2% paraphrase consistency).
• Entropy-Gated Cascade (dgemma -> Gemini Flash, EXP-05): dgemma resolves 72% of traffic at Stage 1 (H_norm < 0.16) and escalates only the 28% high-entropy cases to Gemini, achieving 98.0% raw accuracy (97.17% chance-corrected, 70.16 Composite) at 56% lower compute cost than calling Gemini Flash on every request (62.76 Composite).

3. BENCHMARK 2: jev-decision-index (EXP-12 — 5-Area Capability Suite & HF Leaderboard)
• Evaluates 19 panel benchmarks across Knowledge, Language, Retrieval, Tools, and Arts (72,590 cases; capacity rejections score 0.0).
• Why Standard DiffusionGemma Is #2 (55.56) Instead of #1 (55.74) on the Public Leaderboard:
  Inspecting multimodalart/jev-decision-index telemetry reveals DiffusionGemma already beats #1 Jevfire on Knowledge (54 vs 52) and Language (66 vs 63), trailing overall by just 0.18 pts due to three serving bottlenecks in standard open-jev/vLLM engines:
    (a) 26-Option [A-Z] Ceiling: POP909-CL (129 options) collapses to 0.00% (vLLM HTTP 422) / 2.63% (JoshuaSP), and BANKING77 (77 opts) / CLINC150 (151 opts) are rejected.
    (b) Multi-Slot Canvas Overflow: BRIGHT (32 slots/request) drops to 8.48% on vLLM (ContractNLI has 17 slots, ToolRet has 32 slots).
    (c) Unscaled Logit Overconfidence: 75.8% of raw T=1.0 predictions emit 98.8% confidence on 75.2% accuracy, causing 0.2388 ECE.
• How dgem Solves All Three (EXP-12 Cloud Run L4 Receipt):
  By combining Multi-Slot Canvas Batching (<=8 slots/pass) + 2-Stage Bracket Tournament Routing (27..255 options) + Temperature Scaling (T*=1.25) behind a native POST /v1/systemone endpoint:
    - Structural Coverage rises from 72.7% -> 100.0% (+27.3%)
    - 22-Benchmark Ablation Headline Index jumps from 76.67 -> 98.89 (+22.22 pts)
    - Wide-Option Tournament Accuracy (30-36 opts): 100.0% (~336ms) | Multi-Slot Batch Accuracy (11-12 slots): 94.29% (~301ms)
    - 10-Bin ECE drops to 0.0371 (Brier 0.0184), projecting DiffusionGemma from #2 (55.56) to #1 Open Model (~56.5+) on the full hub.
